Denis Campbell Bray

黎敦義

Denis Campbell Bray (黎敦義) held the post of Secretary for Home Affairs between 1973 and 1984. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1971 and 1984, as an ex officio member and an official member (District Commissioner, New Territories); and sat on the Executive Council from 1973 to 1984 or 1985, as an ex officio member and an official member. He also acted as Chief Secretary three times (1976–1984) and acted as Secretary for Home Affairs once (1970). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ. The LegCo database shows him as Secretary for Home Affairs without a break from 12 November 1973 to 20 December 1984. The Year Books list Li Fook-kow in that post on 2 January 1978, 1979 and 1980, and the database itself has Li Fook-kow as Secretary for Home Affairs from 18 May 1977 to 6 August 1980. We show a break in his service for those years; he is back in the post on the list of 2 January 1981.
