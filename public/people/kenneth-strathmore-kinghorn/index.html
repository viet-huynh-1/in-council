Kenneth Strathmore Kinghorn

景韓

Kenneth Strathmore Kinghorn (景韓) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1960 and 1969, as an official member (District Commissioner, New Territories; Director of Urban Services). He also acted as Commissioner for Labour once (1960).
