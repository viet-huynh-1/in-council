David Akers-Jones

鍾逸傑

David Akers-Jones (鍾逸傑) held the posts of Secretary for the New Territories (1974–1981), Secretary for City and New Territories Administration (1981–1983), Secretary for District Administration (1983–1985) and Chief Secretary (1985–1986). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1973 and 1986, as an ex officio member and an official member (District Commissioner, New Territories); and sat on the Executive Council from 1978 or 1979 to 1986, as an ex officio member and an official member. He also acted as Governor once (1986–1987); acted as Chief Secretary once (1985); acted as Secretary for Home Affairs twice (1982–1983); and acted as Secretary for the Environment once (1973).
