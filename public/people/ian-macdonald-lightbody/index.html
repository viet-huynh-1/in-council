Ian MacDonald Lightbody

黎保德

Ian MacDonald Lightbody (黎保德) held the posts of Secretary for Housing (1972–1977) and Secretary for Administration (1977–1979). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1966 and 1977, as an official member (Commissioner for Resettlement; District Commissioner, New Territories); and sat on the Executive Council from 1977 or 1978 to 1978 or 1979, as an official member. He also acted as Chief Secretary once (1978).
