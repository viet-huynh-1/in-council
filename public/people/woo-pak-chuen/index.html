Woo Pak-chuen

胡百全

Woo Pak-chuen (胡百全) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1964 and 1974, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1972 to 1976 or 1977, as an unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Executive Council 13 times (1966–1970); and sat as a temporary member of the Executive Council once (1971–1972).
