James Jeavons Robson

盧秉信

James Jeavons Robson (盧秉信) held the post of Secretary for the Environment between 1973 and 1976. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1969 and 1976, as an official member (Director of Public Works). He also acted as Secretary for the Environment once (1973) and acted as Director of Public Works twice (1964–1966).
