Guy Mowbray Sayer

沙雅

Guy Mowbray Sayer (沙雅) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1973 and 1974, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1974 or 1975 to 1977 or 1978, as an unofficial member.
