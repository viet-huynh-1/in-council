Lee Kai-ming

李啟明

Lee Kai-ming (李啟明) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1995 and 1997, as a member elected by a functional constituency (Labour); and sat on the Provisional Legislative Council between 1996 and 1997.
