Li Fook-shu

李福樹

Li Fook-shu (李福樹) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1962 and 1968, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1966 and 1968, as an unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council once (1961). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ on when full membership began. The LegCo database shows one continuous period from 7 June 1961. The Year Book list of 1 January 1962 does not include this member; its remarks show a provisional spell standing in for Richard Charles Lee (1 June to 5 September 1961), and the next list records that he succeeded Kwok Chan on 1 July 1962. We show the 1961 spell as provisional and full membership from 1 July 1962.
