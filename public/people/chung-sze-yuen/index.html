Chung Sze-yuen

鍾士元

Chung Sze-yuen (鍾士元) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1968 and 1978, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1972 to 1988 or 1989, as an unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council 11 times (1965–1968) and on the Executive Council once (1970). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ on when full membership began. The LegCo database shows one continuous period from 28 July 1965. The Year Book remarks show only provisional spells, standing in for absent members, before that; the first Year Book list naming this member as a full member is dated 1 January 1969. We show the earlier spells as provisional and full membership from no later than 1 January 1969.
