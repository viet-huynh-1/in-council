Teng Pin-hui

鄧炳輝

Teng Pin-hui (鄧炳輝) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1963 and 1970, as an official member (Director of Medical and Health Services); and sat on the Executive Council between 1966 and 1970, as an official member. He also acted as Director of Medical and Health Services once (1960–1961).
