Michael Leung Man-kin

梁文建

Michael Leung Man-kin (梁文建) held the post of Secretary for Transport between 1987 and 1991. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1984 and 1991, as an official member (Director of Education). He also acted as Secretary for Education and Manpower once (1986); acted as Director of Education five times (1981–1984); and acted as Secretary for Social Services once (1980).
