Wilson Wang Tze-sam

王澤森

Wilson Wang Tze-sam (王澤森) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1968 and 1975, as an appointed unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council twice (1968). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ on when full membership began. The LegCo database shows one continuous period from 20 December 1967. The Year Book remarks show only provisional spells, standing in for absent members, before that; the first Year Book list naming this member as a full member is dated 1 January 1969. We show the earlier spells as provisional and full membership from no later than 1 January 1969.
