John Calvert Griffiths

祁理士

John Calvert Griffiths (祁理士) held the post of Attorney General between 1979 and 1983. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1979 and 1983, as an ex officio member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1979 and 1983, as an ex officio member.
