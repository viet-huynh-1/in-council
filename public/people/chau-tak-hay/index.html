Chau Tak-hay

周德熙

Chau Tak-hay (周德熙) held the post of Secretary for Health and Welfare between 1988 and 1989. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1988 and 1989, as an official member. He also acted as Secretary for District Administration three times (1987–1988) and acted as Secretary for Health and Welfare once (1985).
