William Charles Goddard Knowles

鈕魯詩

William Charles Goddard Knowles (鈕魯詩) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1961 and 1964, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1963 and 1965, as an unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Executive Council once (1962).
