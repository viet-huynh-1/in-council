Li Fook-kow

李福逑

Li Fook-kow (李福逑) held the posts of Secretary for Social Services (1973–1977) and Secretary for Home Affairs (1977–1980). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1972 and 1980, as an ex officio member and an official member (Director of Social Welfare); and sat on the Executive Council from 1976 or 1977 to 1980, as an ex officio member and an official member. He also acted as Secretary for Home Affairs five times (1971–1976) and acted as Financial Secretary once (1968). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.
