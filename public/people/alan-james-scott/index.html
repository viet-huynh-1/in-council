Alan James Scott

施恪

Alan James Scott (施恪) held the posts of Secretary for the Civil Service (1976–1977), Secretary for Housing (1977–1980), Secretary for Information (1980–1981) and Secretary for Transport (1982–1985). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1976 and 1985, as an official member (Official member). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Not on the Year Book list of members on 2 January 1983, although the LegCo database shows him sitting on that date as Secretary for Transport. We follow the database; the printed list may have missed him.

Two short spells (May–July 1980 and December 1981) are labelled "Appointed (Unofficial)" in the members database. He was a serving civil servant at the time, between two Secretary posts, so we show them as official seats.
