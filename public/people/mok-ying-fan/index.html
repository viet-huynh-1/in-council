Mok Ying-fan

莫應帆

Mok Ying-fan (莫應帆) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1995 and 1997, as a member elected by a functional constituency (Urban Council); and sat on the Provisional Legislative Council between 1996 and 1997.
