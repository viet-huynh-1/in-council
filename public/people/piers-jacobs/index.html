Piers Jacobs

翟克誠

Piers Jacobs (翟克誠) held the posts of Secretary for Economic Services (1982–1985) and Financial Secretary (1986–1991). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1982 and 1991, as an ex officio member and an official member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1986 and 1991, as an ex officio member. He also acted as Chief Secretary twice (1989–1990); acted as Financial Secretary once (1985); and acted as Secretary for Economic Services twice (1980–1981).
