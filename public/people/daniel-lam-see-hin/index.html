Daniel Lam See-hin

林思顯

Daniel Lam See-hin (林思顯) appears in these records only as a stand-in: he sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council twice (1967–1968). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ. The LegCo database shows membership from 26 April 1967 to 20 December 1967, but in those years the Year Books name this person only as standing in provisionally for absent members. We show those spells as provisional.
