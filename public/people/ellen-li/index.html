Ellen Li

李樹培夫人

Ellen Li (李樹培夫人) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1966 and 1973, as an appointed unofficial member. She also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council five times (1965–1966). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ on when full membership began. The LegCo database shows one continuous period from 23 June 1965. The Year Book remarks show only provisional spells, standing in for absent members, before that; the first Year Book list naming this member as a full member is dated 1 January 1967. We show the earlier spells as provisional and full membership from no later than 1 January 1967.

Note

Official records list her under her husband's name: 李樹培夫人, "Mrs Li Shu-pui". Her own name was 曹秀群 (Sau-kuan), and as a married woman 李曹秀群.
