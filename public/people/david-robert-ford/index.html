David Robert Ford

霍德

David Robert Ford (霍德) held the posts of Secretary for Information (1980), Secretary for Housing (1985) and Chief Secretary (1986–1993). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1980 and 1993, as an ex officio member and an official member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1986 and 1993, as an ex officio member. He also acted as Governor twice (1989) and acted as Secretary for Housing once (1983). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

The Year Book list of 2 January 1987 calls him Acting Chief Secretary. The LegCo database has him as Chief Secretary from 15 December 1986, and the Hansard of 7 January 1987 styles him "The Honourable the Chief Secretary" without "acting". We follow the database and Hansard.
