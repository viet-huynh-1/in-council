Hugh David MacEwen Barton

Hugh David MacEwen Barton sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1960 and 1962, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1962 and 1963, as an unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Executive Council twice (1960).
