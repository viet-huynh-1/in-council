Gerald Mordaunt Broome Salmon

沙魯民

Gerald Mordaunt Broome Salmon (沙魯民) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1970 and 1972, as an appointed unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council once (1969–1970) and on the Executive Council once (1971). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ on when full membership began. The LegCo database shows one continuous period of membership. The Year Book remarks show that before 2 June 1970 this member sat only provisionally, standing in for absent members, and became a full member on 2 June 1970. We show the earlier spells as provisional.
