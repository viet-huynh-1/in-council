Kenneth Wallis Joseph Topley

陶建

Kenneth Wallis Joseph Topley (陶建) held the posts of Secretary for Education (1981–1983) and Secretary for Education and Manpower (1983). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1974 and 1983, as an official member (Chairman, Committee to Review Post-Secondary and Technical Education; Director of Education). He also acted as Commissioner for Labour once (1964).
