Fung Hon-chu

馮漢柱

Fung Hon-chu (馮漢柱) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1964 and 1970, as an appointed unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council four times (1962–1963). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources differ on when full membership began. The LegCo database shows one continuous period from 8 August 1962. The Year Book lists of 1 January 1963 and 1964 do not include this member; their remarks show only provisional spells standing in for absent members (1962–63). He is listed as a member on 1 January 1965. We show the earlier spells as provisional and full membership from 1964.
