Jeremy Fell Mathews

馬富善

Jeremy Fell Mathews (馬富善) held the post of Attorney General between 1988 and 1997. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1988 and 1995, as an ex officio member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1988 and 1997, as an ex officio member. He also acted as Attorney General nine times (1983–1988) and acted as Law Draftsman once (1980).
