Denys Tudor Emil Roberts

羅弼時

Denys Tudor Emil Roberts (羅弼時) held the posts of Attorney General (1966–1973) and Colonial Secretary (retitled Chief Secretary during 1976) (1973–1978). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1966 and 1978, as an ex officio member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1966 and 1978, as an ex officio member. He also acted as Governor nine times (1974–1978) and acted as Attorney General three times (1963–1965).
