John Rawling Todd

杜迪

John Rawling Todd (杜迪) held the post of Secretary for Housing in 1986. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council in 1986, as an official member. He also acted as Secretary for Lands and Works four times (1984–1986); acted as Secretary for the Environment twice (1980–1981); and acted as Secretary for the New Territories once (1978).
