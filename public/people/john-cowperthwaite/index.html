John Cowperthwaite

郭伯偉

John Cowperthwaite (郭伯偉) held the post of Financial Secretary between 1961 and 1971. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1961 and 1971, as an ex officio member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1961 and 1971, as an ex officio member. He also acted as Financial Secretary once (1960).
