John Charles Creasey Walden

華樂庭

John Charles Creasey Walden (華樂庭) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1977 and 1980, as an official member (Director of Home Affairs). He also acted as Secretary for Home Affairs twice (1978–1979) and acted as Commissioner for Resettlement twice (1971–1972).
