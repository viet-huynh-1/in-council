Graham Barnes

班禮士

Graham Barnes (班禮士) held the posts of Secretary for Lands and Works (1986–1989) and Secretary for Planning, Environment and Lands (1989–1992). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1980 and 1992, as an official member (Regional Secretary (Hong Kong and Kowloon), City and New Territories Administration; Director of Home Affairs). He also acted as Chief Secretary twice (1991) and acted as Secretary for the Environment twice (1977–1979). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.
