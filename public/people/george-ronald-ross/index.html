George Ronald Ross

羅仕

George Ronald Ross (羅仕) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1964 and 1968, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1972 to 1974 or 1975, as an unofficial member. He also sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Legislative Council once (1968) and on the Executive Council 18 times (1965–1971). The sources disagree about some of these dates; see “Where the sources differ” below.

Where the sources differ

Sources agree that he was an unofficial member of LegCo from July 1964 (the Year Book: succeeded W. C. G. Knowles on 1 July 1964; the LegCo database: from 8 July 1964) until Michael Herries succeeded him on 1 July 1968. He then sat provisionally for Herries until September 1968, which is where the database's end date of 11 September 1968 comes from. His other provisional spells in these years were in the Executive Council.
