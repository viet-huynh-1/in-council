Bruce Liu Sing-lee

廖成利

Bruce Liu Sing-lee (廖成利) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1995 and 1997, as a member elected by a geographical constituency (Kowloon Central); and sat on the Provisional Legislative Council between 1996 and 1997.
