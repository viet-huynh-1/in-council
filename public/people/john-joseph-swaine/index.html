John Joseph Swaine

施偉賢

John Joseph Swaine (施偉賢) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1980 and 1995, as President and an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1988 or 1989 to 1991 or 1992, as an unofficial member.
