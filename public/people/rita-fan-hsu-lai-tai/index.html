Rita Fan Hsu Lai-tai

范徐麗泰

Rita Fan Hsu Lai-tai (范徐麗泰) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1983 and 1992, as an appointed unofficial member; sat on the Executive Council from 1988 or 1989 to 1992 or 1993, as an unofficial member; and sat on the Provisional Legislative Council between 1996 and 1997.
