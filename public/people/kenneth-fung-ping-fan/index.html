Kenneth Fung Ping-fan

馮秉芬

Kenneth Fung Ping-fan (馮秉芬) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1960 and 1965, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1962 and 1972, as an unofficial member.
