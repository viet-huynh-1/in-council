John Crichton McDouall

麥道軻

John Crichton McDouall (麥道軻) held the post of Secretary for Chinese Affairs between 1960 and 1966. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1960 and 1966, as an ex officio member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1960 and 1966, as an ex officio member.
