Gerald Hugh Choa

蔡永業

Gerald Hugh Choa (蔡永業) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1970 and 1976, as an official member (Director of Medical and Health Services); and sat on the Executive Council from 1972 to 1976 or 1977, as an official member. He also acted as Director of Medical and Health Services once (1968) and sat provisionally, standing in for absent members, on the Executive Council once (1971).
