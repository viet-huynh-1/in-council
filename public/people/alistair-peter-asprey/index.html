Alistair Peter Asprey

區士培

Alistair Peter Asprey (區士培) held the post of Secretary for Security between 1990 and 1991. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1990 and 1991, as an official member. He also acted as Secretary for Security three times (1989) and acted as Secretary for Lands and Works once (1987).
