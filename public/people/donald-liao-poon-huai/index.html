Donald Liao Poon-huai

廖本懷

Donald Liao Poon-huai (廖本懷) held the posts of Secretary for Housing (1980–1985), Secretary for District Administration (1985–1989) and Secretary for Home Affairs (1989). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1980 and 1989, as an official member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1985 or 1986 to 1989 or 1990, as an official member. He also acted as Secretary for Housing six times (1973–1979).
