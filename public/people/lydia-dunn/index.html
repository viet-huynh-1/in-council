Lydia Dunn

鄧蓮如

Lydia Dunn (鄧蓮如) sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1976 and 1988, as an appointed unofficial member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1982 or 1983 to 1995 or 1996, as an unofficial member.
