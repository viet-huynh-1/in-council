Eric Peter Ho

何鴻鑾

Eric Peter Ho (何鴻鑾) held the posts of Secretary for Social Services (1977–1983) and Secretary for Trade and Industry (1983–1987). He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1977 and 1987, as an official member; and sat on the Executive Council from 1984 or 1985 to 1987 or 1988, as an official member. He also acted as Secretary for Health and Welfare once (1983) and acted as Director of Commerce and Industry twice (1973).
