Edmund Brinsley Teesdale

戴斯德

Edmund Brinsley Teesdale (戴斯德) held the post of Colonial Secretary between 1963 and 1965. He sat on Hong Kong’s Legislative Council between 1963 and 1965, as an ex officio member; and sat on the Executive Council between 1963 and 1965, as an ex officio member. He also acted as Governor once (1964) and acted as Colonial Secretary four times (1960–1962).
