# In Council: Hong Kong, 1960–1997

An interactive visualizer of who sat on Hong Kong's Legislative Council (立法局) and Executive Council (行政局), and who held the colony's senior official posts, on any day from 1 January 1960 to 30 June 1997.

**Site:** https://council.viet-huynh.com (coming soon)
**By:** [Viet Huynh](https://viet-huynh.com)
