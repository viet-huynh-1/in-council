# In Council: Hong Kong, 1960–1997

An interactive visualizer of who sat on Hong Kong's Legislative Council (立法局) and Executive Council (行政局), and who held the colony's senior official posts, on any day from 1 January 1960 to 30 June 1997. The Provisional Legislative Council of 1996–97 has its own tab.

**Site:** https://council.viet-huynh.com
**By:** [Viet Huynh](https://viet-huynh.com)

## What's in it
- Seats on any day, as cards or as a chamber diagram, with acting and provisional members
- Timeline: membership charts, play the years forward, compare two dates
- A page for each of the 424 people, with roles, dates and sources
- Connections between council members and companies and institutions
- A printable poster of any council on any day
- The full dataset as CSV
- A list of what's missing (the site's Limitations page)

## How the site is built
The whole site is one self-contained file, `public/index.html`, with the data inside it. GitHub Actions publishes the `public/` folder to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
